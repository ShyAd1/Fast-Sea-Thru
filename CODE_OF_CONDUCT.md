# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the principles of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
(CC BY 4.0), in a condensed form.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, welcoming and patient with other people.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the community.
- Assume good faith and ask for clarification before reacting.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, trolling, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any other channel related to the project) and when someone
represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer at
**adheva05@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed promptly and treated
confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues
and other contributions, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior
is considered inappropriate.
